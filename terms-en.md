## 1. Purpose of the Skill

The skill is designed to provide prayer times, set up automatic prayer reminders, and deliver religious reminder content.

Key features include:

- Displaying and providing prayer times
- Automatic prayer time reminders (optionally with spoken Adhan/Ezan)
- Playback of MP3 Adhan/Ezan on request
- Delivery of short religious reminder content (for example, paraphrased Hadith)

All content is intended to support daily religious practice and personal reflection.
